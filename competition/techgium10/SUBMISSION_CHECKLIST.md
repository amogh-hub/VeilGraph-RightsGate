## Demo evidence

- [x] Three-minute judge script prepared in `JUDGE_DEMO.md`.
- [x] Clean, ambiguous and blocked behavior is explainable from evidence and policy citations.
- [x] Frozen synthetic results and limitations are checked in.
- [ ] Record and rehearse a portal-specific demo on the presentation machine.
- [ ] Test the offline path, display scaling, audio output and backup video.
- [ ] Use only synthetic, owned or properly licensed demonstration media.

## Claims that must remain qualified

- Synthetic frozen-set metrics are not real-world accuracy claims.
- A candidate match is not infringement, ownership or legal clearance.
- Enrolled image/acoustic matching is not open-world face or speaker recognition.
- Visible-watermark verification does not cover arbitrary invisible watermark families.
- Video audio-track rights analysis and authenticated production CMS delivery remain outside this release.
- No engineering process can guarantee a hackathon win; the defensible advantage is the reproducible evidence and fail-closed product workflow.
